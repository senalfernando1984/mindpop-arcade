How to play Peg Jump

Clear the board one jump at a time. The dream ending is just one peg left.

01🔵Choose a peg

Tap any peg that can leap over a neighbouring peg.

02↗️Jump into a hole

Move in a straight line across exactly one peg into an empty hole.

03✨Watch it vanish

The peg you jumped over disappears. Try to finish with just one!

💡 Stuck? Use Hint for a smart next move, or Undo to try something different.
